Check before every flight

The first seven are automatic in preflight(), which every flying function runs and which refuses to take off on any failure. The rest are people checks.

  • auto

    Battery at rest ≥ 3.5 V, race ≥ 4.10 V

    A full battery before identify and tuning, so runs compare. In flight the loop lands below 3.1 V.
  • auto

    On battery, USB unplugged

    Charging reads a high voltage; preflight wants the power state "in use".
  • auto

    Flow deck attached, height estimate agrees with the sensor

    Catches a loose deck or a range sensor that reads nothing.
  • auto

    Supervisor not locked, can arm or already armed

    Locked after a crash: python crazyflie.py reboot --label 4, a soft reboot over the radio, cleared it where recover=True did not.
  • auto

    Position estimate still on the ground

    Also fails if someone else is moving the drone, or on deep carpet pile (height reads 0): put a thin card under it.
  • auto

    Facing +x, |yaw| ≤ 10° after the estimator reset

    Our loop sends body-frame velocities from world-frame errors. Flight 4_1533 flew at 95° and went sideways.
  • auto

    Abort window works this session

    drone_pipeline.py abort_test, no drone, press Enter within 10 s.
  • by hand

    Floor has texture under the flight area

    python crazyflie.py flow --label 4, hold the drone 0.5 m up and move it. Surface quality should be about 100; near 0 means the estimate will not see drift.
  • by hand

    Nobody else on our radio address

    Neighbouring groups use their own label. Two laptops sending setpoints to one drone means neither controls it, and Enter may not land it.
  • by hand

    Hardware after any crash

    Props uncracked and tight, motors spin freely, flow deck seated, battery strap tight.
  • by hand

    People

    Pilot clicked into the abort window, a second person watching, 1.5 m clear, nobody within arm's length.
  • rule

    Niels says go for that specific flight

    One explicit go per flight. Ground checks need none.

New: fly the drone with the keyboard

The course only has keyboard flying in the 2D simulator. The real-drone loop already pumps a Qt window for the abort key, so a keyboard mode can reuse that window and every safety check of fly(). Built: drone_pipeline.py key_test (no drone) and drone_pipeline.py keyboard speed=0.1 (flies).
