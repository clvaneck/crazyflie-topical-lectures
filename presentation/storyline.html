03 · The two progress slides, beat by beat

6 · “A perfect calibration that made it worse”

  1. Measure. Drone flat on the floor, 60 s at 50 Hz. Two bars on a log axis: measured accelerometer noise 0.011 m/s² against the firmware's 0.5. Label: “45× smaller”.
  2. Upload and fly. 0.5 m forward and back. The real trace draws itself: x overshoots to 0.81 m instead of 0.61 m; y drifts to −0.9 m while the target is 0.
  3. Why. At rest that is sensor noise. In the air the filter's process noise has to cover vibration, air and thrust too. With 45× too little, the filter trusts its prediction and ignores the flow deck.
  4. Decision for tomorrow: race on the firmware defaults.

Challenge in one line: the obvious fix was wrong, and only a real flight showed it.

8 · “Blind for 3 s → slow down”

  1. See. Height trace with the ToF dots. The filter's band hugs the data: σ 0.11 cm.
  2. Go blind. The dots vanish from 8 to 11 s. The band balloons to σ 7.3 cm and the estimate wanders 10.5 cm off. The first returning reading snaps it back.
  3. Shrink the hoop. Face-on hoop, radius 0.5 m. Drone size, survey and tracking margins eat into it, then the 2.5σ ring. Usable clearance 0.22 m → 0.13 m.
  4. Speed. Gauge needle drops from 0.44 to 0.27 m/s. The point line appears.

Challenge in one line: the filter is honest about being unsure, so we have to listen to it.
